Research seminar at **The University of Osaka, 4–6 November 2026**, on GPBiCG(m, ℓ, k) and preliminary strategies for automatic parameter selection.

This public selection covers the three-phase method, live adaptive parameter control, learned initialization, numerical comparisons and the cost of selection. The appendix describes the experimental protocols and machine-learning criteria. The results motivate further research into reliable and efficient numerical solver selection; they do not establish universal solver superiority.
